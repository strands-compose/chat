## Reporting Issues

If you encounter a bug or have a feature request:

1. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/strands-compose/chat/issues) to avoid duplicates
2. Open a new issue with:
   - Clear title describing the problem
   - Steps to reproduce (for bugs)
   - The settings involved, with secrets removed
   - Relevant log lines
   - strands-compose-chat version, Python version, and database (SQLite or Postgres)
